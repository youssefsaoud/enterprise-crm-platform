# Release v0.4.0: Lead Management And Team Assignments

`v0.4.0` marks the Month 4 CRM milestone.

## Included

- Lead schema and lead event history.
- Lead CRUD, search, filters, assignment, status changes, soft deletion, and history APIs.
- Record-level visibility for sales representatives.
- Angular lead list, create/edit form, detail, assignment, status, and history workflows.
- API, database, README, review, and backlog documentation.

## Not Included

- Lead conversion to customers, contacts, or opportunities.
- Opportunity pipeline views.
- Email notifications or lead scoring.
