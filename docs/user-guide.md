# CRM User Guide

This guide summarizes the MVP workflows for business users.

## Sign in and navigation

Open the CRM frontend URL, sign in with the assigned account, and use the left navigation to move between dashboard, companies, contacts, leads, opportunities, and activities.

## Companies and contacts

Use companies to track customer organizations. Use contacts to track people associated with those organizations. Keep company and contact data current before creating leads or opportunities that depend on them.

## Leads

Create leads for early sales interest, assign an owner, update the status as qualification progresses, and review history before conversion.

## Opportunities

Create opportunities for qualified deals. Update the pipeline stage, amount, close date, and owner as the deal progresses.

## Interactions

Record calls, emails, meetings, and notes from the activity area or related CRM records. Use the timeline to understand account history before follow-up.

## Dashboard

Use the dashboard for pipeline totals, lead status summaries, and recent activity. Metrics are scoped by backend role rules.
