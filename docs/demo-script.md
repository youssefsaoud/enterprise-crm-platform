# MVP Demo Script

1. Open the CRM frontend and sign in.
2. Show the dashboard KPIs and recent activity.
3. Create or open a company and review related contacts.
4. Create or open a contact associated with the company.
5. Create a lead, assign it, and update its status.
6. Convert a qualified lead when applicable.
7. Create or open an opportunity and move it through the pipeline.
8. Record an interaction and show it in the timeline.
9. Demonstrate logout and protected route behavior.

Keep the demo focused on end-to-end CRM flow rather than implementation details.
